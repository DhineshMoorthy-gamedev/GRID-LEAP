Step onto the grid. Calculate your path. Make the leap.

Grid Leap is an elegant, minimalist logic puzzle where numbers dictate your movement. Every tile you land on determines the exact distance of your next jump in any cardinal direction (Up, Down, Left, Right). Plan your sequence carefully, avoid dead-end traps, and find the singular path to the Goal.

Designed with an offline-first philosophy and a calm, cerebral aesthetic, Grid Leap delivers deep spatial deduction without timers, rush, or artificial pressure.
