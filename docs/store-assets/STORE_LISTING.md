## 1. App Title & Descriptions

### App Name (Title)

```
Grid Leap - Logic Maze Puzzle
```

### Short Description
```
Leap across the grid. Match the number to your jump distance. Reach the goal!
```

### Full Description
```markdown
Step onto the grid. Calculate your path. Make the leap.

Grid Leap is an elegant, minimalist logic puzzle where numbers dictate your movement. Every tile you land on determines the exact distance of your next jump in any cardinal direction (Up, Down, Left, Right). Plan your sequence carefully, avoid dead-end traps, and find the singular path to the Goal.

Designed with an offline-first philosophy and a calm, cerebral aesthetic, Grid Leap delivers deep spatial deduction without timers, rush, or artificial pressure.

✨ CORE GAMEPLAY & FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧩 100 HANDCRAFTED LEVELS
Progress through five distinct difficulty phases—from intuitive introductory grids to intricate 7x7 labyrinthine challenges. Every single level is mathematically verified for optimal solvability.

📅 PROCEDURAL DAILY CHALLENGES
Test your deduction daily with unique, procedurally generated logic puzzles. Build your daily streak and challenge yourself to find the least-moves solution.

🌓 DUAL MINIMALIST THEMES
Switch effortlessly between two bespoke visual styles:
• Obsidian & Ochre: Deep midnight backdrop with luminous amber accents.
• Vellum & Deboss: Tactile paper aesthetic with soft contrast and crisp debossed typography.

✨ CLEAN & FOCUSED EXPERIENCE
• Interactive Tutorial: Learn the mechanics in seconds with guided interactive lessons.
• Instant Undo & Compass Hints: Smoothly rewind mistaken moves or illuminate the next optimal leap when you need a nudge.
• Tactile Feedback: Responsive animations and haptic feedback accompany every leap.

📴 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
Play anywhere—on commutes, flights, or off the grid. No account creation required, no mandatory internet connection, and your progress stays securely on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎯 HOW TO PLAY

1. Look at the number on your current tile.
2. Swipe or tap in any of the 4 cardinal directions (↑, ↓, ←, →).
3. Your reticle leaps exactly that many tiles forward.
4. Land on new numbers to change your leap power.
5. Reach the GOAL cell without getting trapped!

Can you find the optimal path in the fewest leaps?
Download Grid Leap today and challenge your spatial deduction!
```
