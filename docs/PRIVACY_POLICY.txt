1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Privacy Policy applies to the game "Grid Leap" across all supported platforms
and distribution channels, including:
  • Mobile Applications (Google Play Store / Android)
  • Web Editions (Hosted on Netlify / Browser Platforms)
  • Desktop Editions (Itch.io / Standalone Windows & macOS Builds)
  • Any related services operated by OLAI Digital Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

OLAI Digital Studios is committed to protecting the privacy of our players. 
Grid Leap is designed with an offline-first architecture to provide an enjoyable, 
privacy-respecting puzzle experience. We do not collect, sell, or rent your 
personally identifiable information (PII).


2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grid Leap does NOT require account creation or registration. We do not collect, 
harvest, or store:
  • Real names, physical addresses, or telephone numbers
  • Email addresses (unless you voluntarily contact our support team)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Precise geolocation or GPS coordinates
  • Biometric or financial account details


3. LOCAL ON-DEVICE STORAGE (GAMEPLAY PROGRESS & SETTINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grid Leap stores puzzle progress and user preferences exclusively on your local 
device using standard local storage technologies (e.g., HTML5 localStorage).

This local data includes:
  • Cleared puzzle levels, star ratings, and least-moves records
  • Daily Challenge streak counts and completion timestamps
  • Custom levels created in the Level Designer
  • Player preferences: Audio settings (SFX volume), Haptic feedback state, 
    and Visual themes (Obsidian Dark / Vellum Paper).

This data remains strictly on your local device and is NEVER transmitted to external 
servers or databases. You may reset or erase your data at any time by clearing the 
app's storage/cache in your device or browser settings.


4. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC MONETIZATION & ADVERTISING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To support ongoing game development, Grid Leap utilizes platform-appropriate 
monetization models as outlined below:

A. MOBILE APPLICATIONS (Google Play Store / Android):
   • Advertising (Google AdMob):
     We may display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, or rewarded video 
     advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process pseudonymous 
     device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP addresses, 
     and diagnostic data to deliver contextual or personalized ads and detect fraud.
     - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
     - AdMob Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized ads via your Android 
       device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete/Reset Advertising ID).

   • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing):
     Players may purchase the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade to permanently 
     disable all banner and interstitial advertisements. All financial 
     transactions are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing. 
     OLAI Digital Studios does not receive, process, or store your credit card or 
     banking information.

B. WEB EDITIONS (Netlify / Web Browsers):
   • Web Advertising (Google AdSense):
     The web browser edition may display display advertisements served by Google 
     AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies, web beacons, and device characteristics 
     to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
     - You can manage or opt out of personalized Google web ads at:
       https://adssettings.google.com
     - You can opt out of third-party advertising cookies at:
       https://www.aboutads.info/choices or https://optout.networkadvertising.org

C. DESKTOP EDITIONS (Itch.io / Standalone Builds):
   • 100% Offline & Ad-Free:
     The desktop standalone edition is fully self-contained, serves ZERO 
     third-party advertisements, uses no tracking cookies, and performs no 
     network requests during gameplay.

6. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY (COPPA & GDPR-K COMPLIANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grid Leap is a family-friendly mathematical logic puzzle designed for general 
audiences of all ages. 

We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from 
children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), in accordance 
with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data 
Protection Regulation (GDPR). 

In mobile distributions, ad requests comply with Google Play Families Policy 
requirements, ensuring child-appropriate ad categorization where applicable. If a 
parent or guardian becomes aware that personal data has been inadvertently collected, 
please contact us at olaidigitalstudios@gmail.com, and we will promptly take steps 
to remove such information.


7. INTERNATIONAL DATA RIGHTS (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have specific rights regarding your data:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):
    You have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data held by 
    third-party ad providers, object to processing, and withdraw consent for 
    personalized advertising via the in-game consent dialog or device settings.

  • California & US State Laws (CCPA / CPRA):
    We do not sell personal information or share personal data for monetary 
    value. You have the right to request disclosure of data practices and opt 
    out of cross-context behavioral advertising.

We value your trust in playing our games. Because Grid Leap operates predominantly 
offline and stores your progress locally on your hardware, your gameplay data is 
protected by the built-in operating system security of your individual device.
