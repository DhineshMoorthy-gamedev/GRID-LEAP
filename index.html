PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 2026 • Olai Digital Studios

1. Overview & Offline Commitment

Grid Leap is developed and published by Olai Digital Studios. We are committed to protecting player privacy. Grid Leap is fundamentally architected as an offline-first logic puzzle game. You can play all levels offline without creating an account or logging into any external service.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Grid Leap does not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location/GPS data, or biometric information.

3. Local Device Storage

Your puzzle progress (unlocked levels, star ratings, move counts) and player preferences (visual theme, sound effects, and haptic feedback) are stored exclusively on your device using standard local web storage (localStorage). This data is never transmitted to external servers. You can reset or erase your data at any time by clearing application data or cache in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

In mobile distributions via the Google Play Store, contextual or non-personalized banner advertisements may be served through Google AdMob to support ongoing development. Google AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Players may permanently remove all banner advertisements via the Remove Ads in-app purchase available within Settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

Grid Leap is a family-friendly mathematical puzzle intended for general audiences and logic enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), in compliance with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

6. Developer Inquiries & Support

For questions, support, or privacy requests, please reach out directly to:

Design Philosophy

Grid Leap was created to provide a mindful, distraction-free spatial puzzle experience. By converting every number into a dynamic leap distance, simple grids transform into intricate topological mazes. There are no pay-to-win timers or forced lives penalties in zen play—only pure geometric calculation.

Procedural Engine & Audio

Built with modern web standards, HTML5 Canvas/SVG, and a custom procedural Web Audio synthesizer. All chime chords, bounce sounds, and leap harmonics are generated mathematically in real-time without external audio files.
