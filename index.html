HOW TO PLAY

The Number is the Distance

Whatever number is under your token is exactly how far you leap in any cardinal direction (↑, ↓, ←, →).

Reachable cells glow blue. Plan your jumps to navigate through numbers that lead towards the teal goal cell!

Undo and restart are always free.
