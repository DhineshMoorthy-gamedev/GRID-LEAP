PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 2026 • OLAI Digital Studios

1. Overview & Scope

Grid Leap is developed and published by OLAI Digital Studios (Lead Developer: Dhinesh, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India). We are committed to protecting player privacy across all platforms (Google Play, Web, and Desktop). Grid Leap is architected with an offline-first foundation and does not collect or sell personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Grid Leap does not require an account or registration. We do not collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, contacts, precise GPS location, or financial account details.

3. Local Device Storage

Your puzzle progress (cleared levels, star ratings, move counts), Daily Challenge streaks, custom levels, and player preferences (themes, audio volume, haptics) are stored exclusively on your device via standard local storage (localStorage). This data is never transmitted to external servers.

4. Platform Monetization & Advertising

Mobile (Google Play): May display banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads via Google AdMob (policies.google.com/privacy). Players can permanently disable ads via the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase processed securely by Google Play Billing.

Web (Netlify): May serve display banner ads via Google AdSense. Users can opt out of personalized web ads at adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info.

Desktop (Itch.io / Standalone): 100% offline, self-contained, and completely ad-free with zero third-party tracking.

5. Children’s Privacy

Grid Leap is a family-friendly mathematical puzzle intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), complying with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

6. Developer Inquiries & Support

For questions, support, or privacy requests, please reach out directly to:

Studio: OLAI Digital Studios
Lead Developer: Dhinesh (Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India)
Email: olaidigitalstudios@gmail.com
